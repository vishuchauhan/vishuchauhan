# About Me

I recently completed my **MBA in Finance & Analytics** and am beginning my **Master of Science in Business Analytics (MSBA)** at the **University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC)**.

My work focuses on solving complex business problems by combining **strategy, analytics, financial modeling, and AI**. I enjoy translating quantitative analysis into practical business decisions—from **Go-to-Market strategy and pricing** to **risk modeling, capital allocation, and decision intelligence**.

Before transitioning into analytics, I spent **two years at Maruti Suzuki**, leading quality operations in a high-volume automotive manufacturing environment. Managing production quality, throughput, and cross-functional teams of over 100 people taught me how operational decisions translate into financial outcomes. That **shop-floor-to-top-floor perspective** continues to shape how I approach business problems today.

Today, I build consulting-style analytical frameworks that help organizations make better strategic decisions through rigorous modeling, structured thinking, and data-driven recommendations.
