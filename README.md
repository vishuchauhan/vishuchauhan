## About Me

Having recently completed my **MBA (Finance and Analytics)** and starting my **MSBA at UIUC**, my core focus is solving ambiguous, high-stakes enterprise problems by bridging the gap between advanced quantitative modeling and strategic business execution. 

Before pivoting to advanced data science, I spent two years leading cross-functional teams in heavy industrial manufacturing at Maruti Suzuki. Managing quality, throughput, and a 100+ person workforce on a forging shop floor taught me how to navigate complex organizational dynamics, conduct root-cause analysis, and drive change management. This "shop-floor-to-top-floor" operational reality grounds my analytical work today. 

I don't just build predictive models; I build strategic frameworks. Whether engineering a Go-To-Market (GTM) rollout or designing a quantitative risk system, my objective is to translate rigorous Python analytics into C-suite insights that drive measurable ROI, defend gross margins, and mitigate CapEx risk.

- **Consulting Competencies:** Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy, Operational Value Chain Mapping, CapEx Risk Mitigation, and Data-Driven Decision Frameworks.
- **The Quantitative Edge:** Game Theory (Spence-Harsanyi) applied to B2B pricing, Monte Carlo simulations for probabilistic risk modeling, and predictive multi-agent systems.
- **Beyond the models:** I host the **Business Lens** podcast, where I speak with founders and industry professionals about decision-making, analytics, and innovation.

## Featured Strategy & Architecture

### 1. [Market Entry & GTM Strategy (Predictive Industrial AI)](https://github.com/vishuchauhan/GTM_Consulting_Project)
A comprehensive consulting engagement evaluating a $6B market entry into India's Tier-1 automotive sector. Engineered a 10,000-iteration Monte Carlo ROI simulation and a Game-Theoretic pricing matrix to mathematically defend 85% SaaS gross margins against incumbent price wars, translating heavy Python analytics into an 18-slide executive-ready pitch deck.

### 2. [Strategic Decision Engine (Game-Theoretic Options Trading)](https://github.com/vishuchauhan/Game-Theoretic-Options-Trading-Engine)
A strategic decision-making engine utilizing an LLM-based multi-agent debate framework. Simulates real-world trading floor dynamics by forcing AI agents to debate market regime detection, payoff visualization, and competitor actions before deploying capital.

### 3. [Institutional Risk & Toxicity Filter (Spence-Harsanyi Signaling)](https://github.com/vishuchauhan/microstructural-toxicity-filter)
A quantitative risk-management architecture combining an unsupervised Hidden Markov Model (HMM) regime router with a Game-Theoretic microstructural veto layer. Proved that benchmarking Relative Volume-Weighted Spread (RVWS) against institutional cost proxies successfully filters toxic market anomalies and mitigates historic drawdowns.

### 4. [Alpha Discovery Engine](https://github.com/vishuchauhan/alpha-discovery-engine)
A multi-layer quantitative engine that strips away market noise using Kalman filters. Integrates statistical arbitrage and machine learning to create a transparent, explainable pipeline for asset discovery and automated capital allocation.
